Note: Average Daily Volume (ADV) is calculated by dividing a symbol’s total volume by the number of trading days between the selected ‘Begin Date’ and ‘End Date’. For example, in a monthly ADV query from December 6th, 2019 to December 30th, 2019 there are 17 trading days. The ADV for that month will equal the sum of a symbol’s volume in that range divided by 17. The number of trading days will not change if a symbol was not listed or had a zero-volume day during that interval.
